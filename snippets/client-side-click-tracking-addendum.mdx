Next, set up [client-side click-tracking](/docs/sdks/client-side/features/click-tracking) by setting the `data-domains` property to the custom domain you use for your short links on Dub.

<ClickIdSpoofingPreventionTip />

```html
<script
  src="https://www.dubcdn.com/analytics/script.js"
  defer
  data-domains='{"refer":"yourcompany.link"}' // replace with your referral link domain on Dub
></script>
```

Read the [client-side click-tracking guide](/docs/sdks/client-side/features/click-tracking) for the full setup, including how to add your site's domain to the **Allowed Hostnames** list.
