On [Dub Partners](/help/article/dub-partners), you can offer different incentives for your partners to incentivize them to create high-quality content to spread the word about your product:

| Reward type                                                | Description                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Sale-based (pay-per-sale)](/help/article/sale-rewards)    | This is the most common reward type, where you reward partners for driving sales to your product either via a rev-share or fixed-price commission.    |
| [Lead-based (pay-per-lead)](/help/article/lead-rewards)    | A more effective alternative to pay-per-click, where you reward partners for leads generated (e.g. demo requests, free trial signups, etc.)           |
| [Click-based (pay-per-click)](/help/article/click-rewards) | Rewarding partners for driving clicks to your product – similar to how Google Ads' pay-per-click works.                                               |
| [Partner referral](/help/article/partner-referrals)        | Rewarding partners for referring other influencers, affiliates, and publishers to your program, paid out when the referred partner meets a condition. |
| [Retainer (custom reward)](/help/article/retainer-rewards) | Paying partners a fixed amount on a recurring schedule (e.g. monthly or quarterly), independent of the clicks, leads, or sales they generate.         |

During the program onboarding flow, you'll be asked to create a [default group reward](#default-group-rewards) that will be automatically applied to all new partners who join your program.

This initial reward will be either a lead or a sale-based reward by default, but you can customize the reward terms accordingly.

## Default group rewards

After initial onboarding, you can create additional rewards for clicks, leads, or sales, depending on how you initially set up the group. These rewards will be made available to all partners who join your program.

You can also create [partner referral rewards](/help/article/partner-referrals), which allow partners to earn from bringing on new partners to your program, and [retainer rewards](/help/article/retainer-rewards), which pay partners a fixed amount on a recurring schedule.

If a specific partner or link needs different terms than the rest of the group, you can override the group reward with a [partner-level](#partner-level-rewards) or [link-level](#link-level-rewards) reward.

### Configuring reward types

Depending on the type of reward you're creating, you can choose between a flat fee or a percentage-based commission:

| Reward Type | Model      | Description                                            | Example           |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| **Click**   | Flat       | Partners receive a fixed fee for each click generated  | $2 per click      |
| **Lead**    | Flat       | Partners receive a fixed fee for each qualified lead   | $50 per lead      |
| **Sale**    | Percentage | Partners receive a percentage of sales (revenue share) | 30% of sale value |
| **Sale**    | Flat       | Partners receive a fixed fee for each completed sale   | $50 per sale      |

For sale rewards, you can further customize the recurring nature of the commission:

- One time
- For a set period (anywhere between 3 months to 3 years)
- For the customer's lifetime

### Adding reward conditions

<Tip>
  Customizing reward conditions is only available on the [Advanced plan and
  above](https://dub.co/pricing).
</Tip>

On Dub, you can also set specific requirements for reward completion and can even reward a different type (flat rate or percentage), amount, and duration when they are met. Sale and lead rewards support the full set of conditions, while [click rewards](/help/article/click-rewards#click-reward-conditions) support country-based conditions only. They allow you to set conditions like the following:

- [Geo-specific rewards](/help/article/sale-rewards#geo-specific) (e.g. higher rewards for sales and leads from a specific country)
- [Product-specific rewards](/help/article/sale-rewards#product-spotlight) (e.g. higher rewards for sales of specific products)
- [Partner performance tiers](/help/article/sale-rewards#partner-performance-tiers) (e.g. higher rewards for partners with more than 100 conversions)
- [Staggered reward durations](/help/article/sale-rewards#staggered-reward-durations) (e.g. higher rewards for the first 12 months, then a lower reward for the rest of the customer's lifetime)
- [Sale amount modifiers](/help/article/sale-rewards#sale-amount-modifiers) (e.g. higher rewards for sales over a specific amount)
- [Company size tiers](/help/article/lead-rewards#company-size-tiers) (e.g. higher pay-per-lead rewards for leads from larger companies)
- [Billing cycle rewards](/help/article/sale-rewards#annual-vs-monthly-billing) (e.g. higher rewards for annual plans vs. monthly plans)

Click **Add condition** to set these parameters.

Conditions let you use `AND` or `OR` statements to control how they work. Each condition is tied only to its own reward and doesn't affect others. As shown in the example below, your conditions can be as simple or as complex as you need.

### Metadata conditions

In addition to the built-in attributes, you can create conditions based on any custom `metadata` you attach to your tracking events. This lets you reward partners based on data that's specific to your business (e.g. company size, billing cycle, plan tier, or seat count) that Dub doesn't track natively.

Metadata conditions are available for the `Lead` and `Sale` condition types. To create one, choose `Metadata` as the condition field, enter the metadata field name (the key) you want to match against, then pick an operator and value.

Each metadata condition supports two groups of operators, depending on whether you're matching a text or number value:

| Field type | Operators                                                                                    |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Text**   | `is`, `is not`, `starts with`, `ends with`, `contains`, `does not contain`                   |
| **Number** | `is greater than`, `is greater than or equal to`, `is less than`, `is less than or equal to` |

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/reward-builder-metadata.png"
    alt="Reward builder metadata condition"
  />
</Frame>

You can also attach metadata via the `metadata` prop on the [`/track/lead`](/docs/api-reference/track/lead) and [`/track/sale`](/docs/api-reference/track/sale) endpoints. If you're using our [Stripe integration](/docs/integrations/stripe), sale metadata is automatically pulled from your Stripe [`checkout.session` metadata](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/object#checkout_session_object-metadata) for one-time/subscription checkouts and/or your Stripe [`invoice` metadata](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices/object#invoice_object-metadata) for recurring invoice payments.

For end-to-end examples, see [Company size tiers](/help/article/lead-rewards#company-size-tiers) (lead) and [Annual vs. monthly billing](/help/article/sale-rewards#annual-vs-monthly-billing) (sale).

## Partner-level rewards

<Tip>
  Partner-level and link-level rewards are only available on [Advanced plans and
  above](https://dub.co/pricing).
</Tip>

By default, every partner earns the rewards configured for their [partner group](/help/article/partner-groups). If you want to make an exception without creating a whole new group, you can override the [click](/help/article/click-rewards), [lead](/help/article/lead-rewards), or [sale](/help/article/sale-rewards) reward for a single partner.

Partner-level rewards apply to one partner across all of their links, including links they create later, unless a link has its own [link-level override](#link-level-rewards).

## Link-level rewards

Link-level rewards apply to one link only. They're useful when the same partner runs several campaigns with different economics, for example:

- A **sponsored newsletter placement** that pays a higher rate than the partner's everyday links
- A **conference or event link** with a one-off bonus that shouldn't apply to the partner's other traffic
- A **product-specific landing page** where you want a flat bounty instead of a revenue share

Open the partner's details page from **All Partners**, then click the **Links** tab to see their referral links.

<Frame>

The override modal works the same way as the partner-level one: pick an existing reward from the group, or click **Create reward** to build a new one, then confirm the change with an optional note to the partner.

### How overrides are resolved

When a click, lead, or sale event comes in, Dub picks the reward to apply in this order:

1. **Link-level reward** – If a link-level reward is set for the link that triggered the event, it will be applied.
2. **Partner-level reward** – If no link-level reward is set, the partner-level reward will be applied.
3. **Group reward** – If no partner-level reward is set, the group reward will be applied.

Every partner-level and link-level reward change is recorded in the partner's activity log, including who made the change, the previous and new reward, the link it applied to (for link-level changes), and any note you added. This keeps overrides as auditable as [group reward updates](#group-reward-update-logs).

## Updating a partner group's reward configuration

You can update a [partner group's](/help/article/partner-groups) reward configuration at any time. When this happens, historical commissions won't be updated.

However, the reward configuration for a partner-customer pair will be updated. E.g., if a partner used to be on a one-time reward and was switched to recurring rewards, all subsequent subscription renewals from their **previous referrals** will be eligible for commission again.

To avoid this, instead of editing a given partner group's reward configuration directly, create separate partner groups for one-time vs recurring rewards. This way, when you move partners from one group to the other, the reward configuration for the partner's previous referrals will be preserved.

<Tip>
  When updating a reward, you'll be prompted to [confirm your
  changes](#confirm-reward-creation), and are able to provide optional notes to
  your partners in the update email.
</Tip>
