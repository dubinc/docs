On Dub, you can choose which product tab (Dub Links or Dub Partners) is shown by default when you open your [workspace](/help/article/what-is-a-workspace).

By default, new workspaces open to Dub Links. If your team primarily uses Dub Partners, you can change this so the Partners tab loads first for everyone in the workspace.

Here's how to change the default product in your workspace:

1. Go to your workspace settings by clicking on the gear icon in the sidebar (or visit `https://app.dub.co/<workspace-slug>/settings`).
2. Scroll down to the **Default Product** section.
3. Select the product you want to set as the default from the dropdown.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/workspace/default-product-setting.png"
    alt="Selecting a default product in a Dub workspace"
  />
</Frame>

4. Click **Save changes** to apply your selection.

That's it! The next time you or a teammate opens this workspace, Dub will load the product tab you selected.

<Note>
  Only workspace owners and members with write access can change the default product. Read-only members will see the setting but can't update it.
</Note>
