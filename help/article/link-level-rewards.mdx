---
title: "Link-level rewards"
description: "Learn how to assign a different reward or discount to an individual partner, or to a single partner link, overriding their group defaults."
---

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Advanced plans and
  above](https://dub.co/pricing/partners).
</Tip>

By default, every partner earns the rewards configured for their [partner group](/help/article/partner-groups). If you want to make an exception without creating a whole new group, Dub gives you two levels of override:

- **Partner-level rewards** apply to one partner across all of their links.
- **Link-level rewards** apply to one link only. 

Both let you override the [click](/help/article/click-rewards), [lead](/help/article/lead-rewards), and [sale](/help/article/sale-rewards) rewards, as well as the [discount](/help/article/dual-sided-incentives), independently of one another.

## Setting a partner-level reward

Open the partner's details page from **All Partners**. The **Rewards** card on the right shows the rewards and discount currently in effect for that partner.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-reward-partnerlocation.png" alt="Partner details" />
</Frame>

Hover over a reward and click the **edit** button to open the override modal for that reward type. To change the discount, click the **edit** icon on the discount row instead.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-reward-partnerlocation-edit.png" alt="Partner edit" />
</Frame>

The modal lists every reward of that type in the partner's group, with the group default marked and shown first. Select a different reward to override it for this partner, or click **Create reward** to build a new one on the spot using the standard [reward builder](/help/article/partner-rewards#creating-a-reward).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-discount-edit.png" alt="Partner details" />
</Frame>

Before saving, you'll be asked to [confirm the change](/help/article/partner-rewards#confirm-reward-creation) and can add an optional note to the partner.

Once set, the partner-level reward applies to all of that partner's links, including links they create later, unless a link has its own [link-level override](#setting-a-link-level-reward).

## Setting a link-level reward

Link-level rewards are useful when the same partner runs several campaigns with different economics, for example:

- A **sponsored newsletter placement** that pays a higher rate than the partner's everyday links
- A **conference or event link** with a one-off bonus that shouldn't apply to the partner's other traffic
- A **product-specific landing page** where you want a flat bounty instead of a revenue share
- A **limited-time promotion** with a deeper customer discount tied to one link only

Open the partner's details page from **All Partners**, then click the **Links** tab to see their referral links.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/create-reward-link.png"
    alt="Partner links tab"
  />
</Frame>

Click the `⋯` button to select which reward or discount to edit for this selected link.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/create-reward-linklevel.png"
    alt="Partner links dropdown"
  />
</Frame>

The override modal works the same way as the partner-level one: pick an existing reward from the group, or click **Create reward** to build a new one, then confirm the change with an optional note to the partner.

<Tip>
  You can also set link-level rewards when you [create a link for a
  partner](/help/article/managing-program-partners#links). The **New partner
  link** modal has reward and discount selectors for each type.
</Tip>

## Removing an override

Open the override modal for the reward type and select the **group** reward. 

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-reward-remove.png"
    alt="Removing override"
  />
</Frame>

Before deleting a reward that's used as an override, switch the affected partners or links back to the group reward or to another reward so they don't lose their payout.

## What the partner sees

Partners see their effective rewards on their program **Overview** page in the partner dashboard, and can select from the dropdown to view their other links and associated rewards.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-discount-partner-overview.png"
    alt="Partner overview"
  />
</Frame>

On their **Links** page, each link card shows the primary reward for that link, and a **+N** badge expands to show every reward and discount that applies to it, so partners always know which link pays what.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-discount-partner-links.png"
    alt="Partner links"
  />
</Frame>

When you change a partner-level or link-level reward, the partner also receives an email notification with the updated reward and any note you included, the same as when you [update a group reward](/help/article/partner-rewards#updating-a-partner-groups-reward-configuration).

## Discount overrides

Discounts work the same way at both levels. Edit the discount from the partner's **Rewards** card to change it for the whole partner, or choose **Edit discount** from a link's menu to change it for that link only.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/dub-partners/linklevel-discount-edit.png"
    alt="Discount selection"
  />
</Frame>

If the partner has [Stripe discount codes](/help/article/dual-sided-incentives#option-2-using-stripe-promo-codes-no-code-required), Dub automatically remaps their discount codes to the new discount so the customer-facing coupon matches what the partner or link advertises.

## How overrides are resolved

When a click, lead, or sale event comes in, Dub picks the reward to apply in this order:

1. **Link-level reward** - The single link that triggered the event
2. **Partner reward** - Every link belonging to that partner
3. **Group reward** - Every partner in the group

## Audit trail

Every partner-level and link-level reward or discount change is recorded in the partner's activity log, including who made the change, the previous and new reward, the link it applied to (for link-level changes), and any note you added. This keeps overrides as auditable as [group reward updates](/help/article/partner-rewards#group-reward-update-logs).
