A staging workspace lets you test links, conversion events, integrations, and partner payouts without changing your live analytics or moving real money.

## Staging, sandbox, and production

Dub workspaces run in one of three environments:

- **Staging.** Automatically paired with your production workspace. Use it to test a partner program.
- **Sandbox.** An independent workspace, not paired with production. Sandbox workspaces are not created automatically. [Contact us](https://dub.co/contact/support) if you need one.
- **Production.** Processes real events and potentially real payouts.

### Who gets a staging workspace

Staging workspaces are available on [Business plans and above](https://dub.co/pricing), and only when the workspace has a partner program. Dub creates one when you upgrade to a Business plan (or higher) with a partner program, or when you create a partner program on one of those plans. Free and Pro workspaces, and workspaces without a partner program, don't get a staging workspace.

## Open your staging workspace

Open the workspace menu in the top left corner and click **Switch to staging**. To go back to your production workspace, click **Exit staging** in the banner or the workspace menu.

If **Switch to staging** is missing, check [who gets a staging workspace](#who-gets-a-staging-workspace).

While you're in staging, an amber banner at the top of the dashboard reminds you that no real money or payouts are involved.

Your staging workspace is set up with:

- Name: `{Your workspace name} (Staging)`
- Slug: `{your-workspace-slug}-staging`
- Short link domain: `{your-workspace-slug}.staging.dub.link`
- A copy of your partner program, including its destination URL, branding, application form, landing page, and default group settings

## What stays in sync

Production and staging stay paired, but only workspace settings and members are copied across. Test data stays in staging.

| Data                           | In your staging workspace                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Workspace name, slug, and logo | Synced from production. The staging name is `{Your workspace name} (Staging)` and the slug ends in `-staging`.            |
| Plan and billing period        | Synced from production. Billing stays on the production workspace.                                                        |
| Members and roles              | Invitations, role changes, and removals are managed in production and synced here. API-only machine users are not copied. |
| Links                          | Separate from production.                                                                                                 |
| Partners                       | Separate from production.                                                                                                 |
| Customers                      | Separate from production.                                                                                                 |
| Conversion events              | Separate from production.                                                                                                 |
| Rewards                        | Separate until you [copy them to live](#copy-rewards-and-discounts-to-live).                                              |
| Discounts                      | Separate until you [copy them to live](#copy-rewards-and-discounts-to-live).                                              |

API keys, publishable keys, webhooks, and integration connections are also separate. Create them in the staging workspace when you test.

## Limitations

### Payments and integrations

- Send real payouts. Payouts are simulated and never go through Stripe, PayPal, or Tremendous.
- Connect live Stripe. You can connect a Stripe account in test mode or a [Stripe sandbox](https://docs.stripe.com/sandboxes). Live mode is rejected.

### Workspace administration

- Set staging as your default workspace.
- Edit the workspace name, slug, or logo. Those fields [sync from production](#what-stays-in-sync).
- Manage members. Invitations, invite links, pending invites, roles, and removals are managed in production.
- Delete staging on its own. Deleting your production workspace also deletes its staging workspace.

### Billing and usage

- Change your plan or payment methods. Billing stays on production.
- Staging uses trial usage limits instead of your plan's limits, and staging usage isn't billed.

### Data synchronization and notifications

- Keep rewards and discounts in sync. They stay in staging until you [copy them to live](#copy-rewards-and-discounts-to-live).
- Receive usage emails or application reminders. These are skipped.
- Your staging partner landing page shows the staging banner and is excluded from search results.

## Get your staging credentials

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a staging API key">
  While in your staging workspace, go to **Settings** > [**API Keys**](https://app.dub.co/settings/tokens) and create a new key.

API keys created in staging start with `dub_test_`. Production API keys start with `dub_`. Use that prefix to tell them apart before you deploy. Sandbox API keys use the same `dub_test_` prefix.

</Step>

<Step title="Copy your staging publishable key">
  Publishable keys use a separate format. They start with `dub_pk_` in staging and in production. The prefix is the same in both workspaces, so keep each key in its own environment variable.

Go to your staging workspace's [Tracking settings page](https://app.dub.co/settings/tracking) and copy the [publishable key](/docs/api-reference/authentication#publishable-keys). You'll need it for the [Dub Analytics script](/docs/sdks/client-side/introduction) and the [mobile SDKs](/docs/sdks/client-side-mobile/introduction).

</Step>

<Step title="Point your test environment at staging">
  Use the staging API key and publishable key in your test or staging environment. Events tracked with these keys only show up in your staging workspace.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Testing use cases

Here are the most common flows to test in your staging workspace before going live.

### Track clicks, leads, and sales

Set up conversion tracking with your staging keys using [server-side tracking](/docs/quickstart/server), [client-side tracking](/docs/quickstart/client), or the mobile SDKs ([Swift](/docs/sdks/client-side-mobile/installation-guides/swift) or [React Native](/docs/sdks/client-side-mobile/installation-guides/react-native)).

Then, click one of your staging short links, sign up, and make a purchase. The click, lead, and sale – along with the new customer – show up in your staging workspace's analytics only.

### Stripe

Install the [Dub Stripe app](/docs/integrations/stripe) in Stripe test mode or a [Stripe sandbox](https://docs.stripe.com/sandboxes), and connect it to your staging workspace.

Complete a test payment with one of Stripe's [test cards](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#cards). The payment shows up as a sale in your staging workspace.

<Note>
  Live Stripe mode can't be connected to a staging workspace. Use your
  production workspace when you're ready to track real payments.
</Note>

### Shopify

Install the [Dub Shopify app](/docs/integrations/shopify) on a Shopify development store and connect it to your staging workspace. Test orders from that store are recorded as sales in staging only.

### HubSpot

Connect a HubSpot test account to your staging workspace with the [HubSpot integration](/docs/integrations/hubspot). Leads and closed-won deals from that account are tracked in staging only.

### Partner payouts

Confirm a payout from your staging partner program. Staging payouts:

- Use a simulated payment method, so there's no bank charge and no money transfer
- Show **Payouts simulated** for partners and a **Test** badge on the payout

Programs that use [external or hybrid payouts](/docs/partners/external-payouts) also send [`external_payout.confirmed`](/docs/webhooks/events/external-payout-confirmed) from staging. Create a webhook for that event in your staging workspace and point it at a test handler. The payload matches production, so use an endpoint that records the payout without transferring funds. A 2xx response marks the payout completed. Until your handler responds, the payout stays processing.

<Warning>
  Partners still receive the payout email for staging payouts. Use test partner
  email addresses to avoid notifying real partners.
</Warning>

### Copy rewards and discounts to live

Once you're happy with a reward or discount in staging, open the partner group in your staging workspace and click **Copy to live**. Then, pick a group in your production program that doesn't already have that type of reward or discount.

Copying a Stripe discount also creates the matching coupon on your production workspace's connected Stripe account.

## Go live

When testing looks right, move your application onto production credentials and confirm a real event lands in production.

<Warning>
  **Do not use staging credentials in production.** A `dub_test_` API key, or
  the publishable key copied from staging, sends events to your staging
  workspace. Those events stay out of production analytics, commissions, and
  payouts.
</Warning>

<Steps>
<Step title="Return to your production workspace">
  Click **Exit staging** in the banner or the workspace menu.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify your production API key">
  In your production workspace, go to **Settings** > [**API
  Keys**](https://app.dub.co/settings/tokens) and copy a production key. It
  starts with `dub_`. Replace every `dub_test_` key in your server environment
  with that key.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify your production publishable key">
  On your production workspace's [Tracking settings
  page](https://app.dub.co/settings/tracking), copy the publishable key and
  replace the staging `dub_pk_` value in your client and mobile apps. Both keys
  share the `dub_pk_` prefix, so confirm the value came from production.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify live integration connections">
  Connect your live Stripe account, and any Shopify or HubSpot account you use
  in production, to your production workspace. Leave Stripe test mode and
  development stores on staging.
</Step>

<Step title="Send a real test conversion">
  Click a production short link and complete a signup or purchase with your production keys. Confirm the click, lead, or sale appears in your production workspace's analytics before you treat the integration as live.
</Step>
</Steps>
